How a gap is flagged

A sprint is flagged when a process group has product-owner activity and no UX activity within ±1 sprint of it. The tolerance is deliberate — design and research rarely land in the exact sprint they inform. Change the tolerance if it is flagging noise, but change it in the open.

Why stream isn't a colour

Streams are already encoded by position — each is a labelled lane. Colour is spent on the one distinction the gap check depends on, PO vs UX, which is validated to stay separable for colour-blind readers and in both themes.

The ops lane measures something else

Licences, ride-alongs and access requests are enablement items, not process groups being redesigned. They share the timeline because they gate research access, but they carry no PO/UX tracks and are excluded from the gap check.

S7 is not a full sprint

Dec 21 – Jan 01 is marked reduced capacity. Nothing with a hard commitment should be planned to land there; two process groups currently cross it.
