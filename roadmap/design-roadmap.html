How a gap is flagged

A sprint is flagged when a process group has product-owner activity and no UX activity within ±1 sprint — counting UX work on any horizon. Future-state research therefore counts as coverage for MVP product-owner work, which is why overlapping workshops no longer read as holes.

The row hierarchy is a variable

Every activity carries several dimensions — process group, horizon, design stage, track, owner — and any of them can be the top level, with process groups nested underneath. Process group is the default because it is the unit of work. Whichever dimension you pick, rows carry an also in tag naming the group's other values, so a group appearing in several sections never reads as orphaned.

What counts as a milestone

A transfer or a commitment — not a task finishing. Requirements baselined, design handoff, stakeholder demo, UAT start, PoC gate, discovery synthesis reaching the backlog, go-live. “Wireframes done” is an activity ending, so it isn't one.

Where discovery feeds delivery

The dashed ⇥ milestone is the handoff you can actually point at: discovery synthesis landing n items in the backlog, which MVP delivery then draws from. Three process groups have one inside this window.

S7 is not a full sprint

Dec 21 – Jan 01 is reduced capacity. Nothing with a hard commitment should land there.

Enablement measures something else

Licences, ride-alongs and access requests are enablement items, not workflows being redesigned. They share the timeline because they gate research access, but carry no PO/UX tracks and are excluded from the gap check.
