About this page

How to read the roadmap and the rules it applies.

How a gap is flagged

A sprint is flagged when a process group has product-owner activity and no UX activity within ±1 sprint — counting UX work on any horizon. Future-state research therefore counts as coverage for MVP product-owner work, which is why overlapping workshops no longer read as holes.

Grouping versus filtering

Grouping rearranges every activity and nothing leaves the board; it lives on the board. Filtering removes activities; it lives in the Filters panel with a count of what it took out. Detail and Zoom change how the board is drawn, so they sit with grouping.

The row hierarchy is a variable

Every activity carries several dimensions — process group, horizon, design stage, track, owner — and any can be the top level, with process groups nested underneath. Rows carry an also in tag naming the group's other values, so a group appearing in several sections never reads as orphaned.

What counts as a milestone

A transfer or a commitment — not a task finishing. Open Milestone types for the full list, the definition of each, and how many are in the plan.

Where discovery feeds delivery

The dashed ⇥ milestone is the handoff you can point at: discovery synthesis landing n items in the backlog, which MVP delivery then draws from.

S7 is not a full sprint

Dec 21 – Jan 01 is reduced capacity. Nothing with a hard commitment should land there.

Enablement measures something else

Licences, ride-alongs and access requests are enablement items, not workflows being redesigned. They share the timeline because they gate research access, but carry no PO/UX tracks and are excluded from the gap check.
