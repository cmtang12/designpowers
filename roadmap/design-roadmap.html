Milestones & the gap rule

How a UX coverage gap is flagged

A sprint is flagged when a process group has product-owner activity and no UX activity within ±1 sprint of it — counting UX work on any horizon, so future-state research covers MVP product-owner work.

The tolerance exists because design and research rarely land in the exact sprint they inform. Gaps are computed from the whole plan, so filtering never makes one disappear.

For the team to decide

Is ±1 sprint the right tolerance? ±0 flags far more and creates noise; ±2 will miss real holes. And should a product-owner activity like “backlog grooming” require UX cover at all, or only the ones that commit scope?

Milestone types

A milestone is a transfer or a commitment — something changes hands, or someone locks something in. A task finishing is not one.

For the team to decide

Which of these belong on the roadmap? Not currently included: a data-migration cutover per process group, a training-complete gate before go-live, and security or accessibility sign-off — each is a commitment by someone outside the team, so each passes the test. Deliberately excluded: “wireframes complete”, “research readout”, “sprint review” and “design review” — activities ending or recurring ceremonies, where nothing changes hands.
